Water Level Controller

Automatic motor control with protection

Starts the motor when the tank is low and stops it when the tank is full. It also protects your motor from bad voltage, overload and dry running. Tap a model to see it work.

Wired · 1 motorDJ-W1

    Protection for your motor

    High & low voltage

    The motor stops when the voltage is too high or too low (for example above 270 V or below 180 V). It starts again by itself when the voltage is normal.

    Overload & jammed motor

    If the motor takes too much current, it is stopped at once. This saves the motor winding from burning.

    Dry run

    No water coming? The controller sees it from the motor current. It stops the motor, waits, and tries again up to 3 times.

    Maximum run time

    If the motor runs for too long (for example a burst pipe or a leaking tank), it is stopped.

    Too many starts

    Stops the motor from switching ON and OFF again and again. A start delay also protects it after a power cut.

    Sensor & radio check

    If a float or a wireless tank unit stops working, the motor is stopped safely and the display shows why.

    All protections work in Auto and in Manual mode. The display shows the voltage, current and the last faults.

    Two easy modes

    AUTO

    The motor starts when the tank is low and stops when it is full. After a power cut, the controller always starts in Auto.

    MANUAL ON · AUTO OFF

    Press to fill the tank before a power cut. The motor stops by itself when the tank is full. After 30 minutes, the controller goes back to Auto.

    Options

    • 230 V solenoid valve control
    • Solar-powered tank units (wireless models)
    • Big 4-line display: volts, amps, tank and motor status
